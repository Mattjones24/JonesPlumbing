How this board works

  • The board sorts and summarizes. The owner makes every call himself and decides the order.
  • The board never contacts a customer, sets a price, or sends an invoice.
  • Any message mentioning leaks, bursts, flooding, no heat, or gas is flagged red, whatever its rank.

All voicemails above are sample data written for this project. Names, addresses, and numbers are placeholders. No real customer information appears on this page.
